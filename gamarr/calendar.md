# Calendar

The calendar tab allows you to see any upcoming games (or recently released) that are presently monitored in your library. It is viewable week by week.

The calendar displays:

- Upcoming game releases you're monitoring
- Recently released games
- Games with DLC or updates releasing soon

You can click on any game in the calendar to view its details or initiate a search.
